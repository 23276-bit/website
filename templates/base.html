Instructions:

Here, you can see different underwater hockey players on cards and their attributes as well as some facts about them

These attributes include:

Name (The name of the player)

Cap Number (What number they play as)

Age (How old the player is)

Position (What position they play)

Stick (What model stick they play with)

Ghost (Whether the stick is ghost or not (has holes))

Stick Type (What type of stick it is e.g. soft)

Club (What club the player plays for)
